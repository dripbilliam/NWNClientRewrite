# Gameplay and world behavior

The server owns simulation and results in multiplayer. The replacement submits original requests and renders received movement, combat and effects. Full native offline rules, VM/scheduler and save/load are later work.

## Movement and travel

Click movement, keyboard drive, prediction/collision, gait, camera controls and several original-server door/return routes work. Indexed collision is now the ordinary default; legacy remains a diagnostic path.

Use active movement categories and server rules. Ordinary stock PC running at 4 m/s is not a universal maximum. Keep received coordinates, prediction and displayed support height separate. Bridge entry/deck/exit fixture evidence does not prove every actual placement, BSP tie or idle-height projection.

Complete latency corrections, transformed/overlapping surfaces, shape/remote movement, physical devices and exact targeting. Open-door picking has real action evidence; it does not accept all portal, trigger, teleport or zone-edge travel.

## Combat and effects

Native attack/cast selection, queues, original clips, hit events and private model lifetimes are connected. Player cast-base and resting-path handoffs have real original-server evidence. Bow/sling flight, selected Deflect Arrows, blood, parry and shield impacts have scoped passes.

Broad NPC, shape, multi-attack, interruption, blending and prolonged encounters remain open. A private constructor's animation predicate must not fabricate canonical NPC classes or other undisclosed state. The retained NPC encounter failed before combat stimulus on IPC; it proved no NPC action outcome.

Special attacks include working first-action cases, but intermittent missing Stunning Fist and the Stun→cancel→Quivering Palm chain remain unresolved. Equipped-weapon/eye-effect components do not substitute for actual server/pixel acceptance. Preserve literal native animation events; do not blanket-deduplicate repeated entries.

Death/reaction, corpse, death-options/respawn, resurrection and full rest/recovery workflows still require implementation/acceptance. A rest request sender alone does not close recovery.

## Interactions

Inventory, equipment, containers, merchants, item powers, conversations, quickbars and player barter have working original-server paths. Complete refusals, quantities/stacks, nested containers, paired equipment, range/control changes and mixed-client results.

Associate inventory currently permits open/close/page but restricts item/gold/context/container operations. Complete only the actions allowed for that associate/control role, with server results and stale-owner guards.

The [interface guide](interface.md) covers menus and gestures. The [parity ledger](parity.md) maps these scopes to NWN-22–30 and NWN-41–46. Use independent inventory/character/export witnesses rather than trusting a button click or successful packet send.
