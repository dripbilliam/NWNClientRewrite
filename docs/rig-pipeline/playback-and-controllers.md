# Rig playback and local controllers

This lane follows each request into per-instance playback and ends at the local Part or Node3D field update. The stock source is the pinned x86-64 `nwmain.exe`, SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. Native addresses are PE virtual addresses. The stock render-tree ledgers retain decoded direct callsites and receiver checks. Local source and prior owned-server recordings establish the NWNAnalog path and timings. ## Stock request and run lifetime

1. The creature tick chooses base movement/idle/combat changes through `SetBaseAnimation` `0x140764300`. Gait restoration, timers and conditional transitions can request playback. The ordinary idle branch can finish without issuing the request. At `0x140765444` it dispatches animation-base slot `+0x80` on the object at creature `+0x78`. Base kind `0` reaches `0x140966b40`; Head `1` and TW `11` reach `0x14099a300`; Wield `2` reaches `0x14099cc20`; HeadWield `3` reaches `0x14099dfc0`; Parts `4` reaches `0x14099f8d0` through the recorded adjustors. These wrappers decide which auxiliary Gobs participate, can fall back to `default`, and delegate down the body chain. The base body chooses a name, can use `pause` for an unrecognized code, and invokes the Gob at animation-base `+0xc8` through `+0x30` at `0x140966d34`. [Wrapper, callsite and argument evidence](../../godot-client/.cache/pipeline-rig-repeat-gates-1795282299362/native-dispatch-evidence.json) · [Animation-base ownership](../../godot-client/tools/stock-render-tree/creature-transform-model-publication.md)
2. `Gob::PlayAnimation` `0x140052580` clears its runs for a null, empty or `NULL` name. For an admitted name it resolves the authored clip against its model references, computes position scale, creates or reorders the run, and configures timing, playback flags, weight and fade. Start/done callbacks are possible in the request body. The same-name fast return on the current run requires incoming flag `0x200`; it ORs `0x201` into the run before returning. The ordinary base-setter supplies `0` or `2`, forwarded by its wrappers, and the body `pause` arm supplies zero. Its upstream decision gate is therefore significant even though the lower flag-specific fast return exists. [Stock request body `ev_225949b5bdbee8bf34c101b1ae97e2fffca1c9a239e3ed372e17764bfa5384b7`](../../godot-client/tools/stock-render-tree/original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md#scene-gob-and-part-order) · [Playback guard evidence](../native-pipeline-comparison.md#original-base-playback-dispatch-and-repeated-mixed-client-requests)
3. `Gob::Reset` `0x140053110` rescales retained runs, restores the position-scale factor, applies the active model tree at time zero and weight one, and emits `GobReset`. Visibility-driven `Gob::SetLODModel` `0x140094b90` can swap prebuilt Model and Part roots, update the current level and then enter Reset. Ordinary per-frame advancement consumes the active root at Gob `+0x90`. `Gob::~Gob` `0x1400889c0` raises `Dying`, uses `PlayAnimation(NULL)` to clear runs, removes scene membership and releases Parts and model/callback ownership. [Reset, LOD and active root](../../godot-client/tools/stock-render-tree/original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md#scene-gob-and-part-order) · [Teardown](../../godot-client/tools/stock-render-tree/original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md#skin-pose-draw-and-lifecycle)

The current investigation re-read the preferred-VA bytes from the SHA-matched PE. The creature dispatch at `0x140765444` starts `FF 90 80 00 00 00`, the body request at `0x140966d34` starts `41 FF D0`, the external/behavior/subwindow entries at `0x14008c09e`, `0x140054bb9` and `0x140202bb1` start `FF 50 28`, and the callback at `0x140050e38` starts `FF D0`. Preferred pointers stored at `0x140d88a28`, `0x140d88a30` and `0x140d88c98` resolve to `Gob::Animate` `0x14004dbd0`, `Gob::PlayAnimation` `0x140052580`, and `Gob::DoEvent` `0x140050d50`. The saved Ghidra body evidence supplies the receiver and register provenance around those instructions. ## NWNAnalog comparison and work ledger

The normal creature route feeds `CombatPresentation`, `CombatAnimation` and the `CombatRigs` bridge into an actor's `ModelRigs` owner. Its rig construction enrolls only animation-bearing models in `animated_models`, retains the per-part hierarchy under Node3D, and supplies `follow`/side maps, defaults and metadata. `ModelRigs._model` installs a private `AnimationPlayer` in manual process mode, and `_play` materializes the selected clip on demand through the clip index. Short-lived effect callers can explicitly create a `native_owner` through `play_native`. That route and the opt-in skeleton are separate from the measured ordinary creature animation. [Rig construction](../../godot-client/systems/native_render/rig_construction.gd#L42) · [Combat presentation bridge](../../godot-client/systems/native_render/combat_presentation.gd#L14) · [Private clip and player ownership](../../godot-client/systems/model_rigs.gd#L335) · [Explicit native owner](../../godot-client/systems/model_rigs.gd#L246)

In the client world callback, `apply_poses()` supplies movement, turn corrections, speed and remaining-path base inputs before `combat.update`. Received cast or attack records wait for the right actor lifetime and take precedence over local movement cues. `CombatAnimation.update` uses its own elapsed clock for queued cue durations, restores gait/base at the documented handoff, and resolves queued action side, duration and speed before `play`. `AnimationQueue` orders by day/time and keeps per-record identity, so otherwise identical attack records remain distinct. The independently scheduled rig callback advances the model's Godot playhead using engine delta. This is a two-owner queue/cue and pose lifecycle, even on frames when the numeric base animation is unchanged. [World order](../../godot-client/systems/native_render/live_world.gd#L819) · [Receive/priority/lifetime gates](../../godot-client/systems/native_render/combat_presentation.gd#L27) · [Cue ownership](../../godot-client/systems/combat_animation.gd#L350) · [Queue arithmetic](../../godot-client/systems/animation_queue.gd#L29)

Stock `MdlNode::InternalControl` `0x140051c50` uses the model's authored `NewController` records and the scalar, vector or quaternion interpolator for the destination Part field. Quaternion control uses spherical interpolation. Its optional trace-run branch invokes `NewController::ReportKeys` `0x140052e20` and can publish a `ReportKey` callback with companion position/orientation values through `0x140052c50`. This diagnostic/trace route reads frame-global `DeltaT`, while normal scene advancement uses the `+0x60` snapshot delta. The TriMesh/Skin, Camera, Light and Reference controller entries first check their expected Part type before common control. AnimMesh `0x140051e60` also runs changed-only dynamic vertex/UV publication through `0x1400bd540`. `PartDanglyMesh::Animate` `0x14004ef50` and the emitter controller own separate subtype effects and publication. A replay that matches ordinary local poses alone does not establish parity on those specialized routes. ## Coverage and remaining unknowns

- The pinned direct-edge ledgers verify the clock, entry dispatch, run producer, sparse hierarchy, controller indirection, cue dispatch, and local-result boundaries. The exact request/flag and callback mix for a live scenario has not been counted. Constructor-backed callback and subtype registrations are bounded by the existing static census. Caller-provided hook targets, current authored subtype selection, and precise side effects beyond the mapped families remain conditional. - Existing profiles bracket active advancement and the surrounding scopes. They do not resolve interpolation, duplicate track defaults, mixer setup, dirty propagation, and cue interval CPU into independent savings. A direct native gate-by-gate frequency comparison would need a matched capture of equivalent actor and server work. This investigation used saved evidence and performed no new runtime tests.
