# NWN-73 handoff: existing particle geometry worker

Current source is opt-in via `--particle-presentation=shared --particle-geometry-worker=on`. `particle_stream_group.gd` passes `geometry_worker=true` to native dispatch and disables every emitter's separate `_process`/surface route in strict mode. Unsupported owners register a `suppressed_fallback_tick`, and an emitter outside the group cannot silently render on the old path (`particle_emitter.gd`). In ordinary shared mode the existing fallback still runs. A `reason()` rejection can come from reference/custom owner, instance-stream rollback, scene lighting, fog, custom collision service, or unavailable wind owner. The live `particle-geometry-on-l` report has `geometry_worker=true`, 1,678 worker frames at its sampled point, `fallback_reasons={}` and `suppressed_fallbacks=0`; that proves that observed scope used the worker, not universal effect coverage.

In `native/particle_presentation.hpp:185-240`, eligibility, source/readiness, actor clock, `Step.advance` (controls, channel/pose sampling, simulation/RNG), sorting, range sizes and material grouping run in the established serial order. At lines 241-289, chunks retain main-thread geometry callbacks; nonchunk jobs take disjoint byte ranges, split by vertex count across one persistent worker and the calling thread, join, validate every range's written count and publish the packet only after completion. `particle_geometry_worker.hpp` keeps job storage caller-owned, propagates worker exceptions, and joins its thread on destruction. `refresh_visibility` reuses the last complete canonical packet and does not advance clocks/RNG/physics. This proves the intended ordering boundary from source; it does not prove every effect's output parity.
