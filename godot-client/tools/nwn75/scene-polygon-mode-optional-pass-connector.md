## Shadow-volume pair

`Scene::RenderShadows` `0x1400d0ab0` enters the pair only on its `param_1 != 0` branch, when `g_nRenderShadowVolumes` is **1 or 3** (`((value-1)&0xfffffffd)==0`). The outer `g_nRenderShadowVolumes != 0` gate is also present. It calls `RenderInterface::SetPolygonRenderMode(1)` at `0x1400d0d7e` before the shadow-volume render sequence, then `SetPolygonRenderMode(2)` at `0x1400d0e57` after it. The separate `param_2` controls an earlier stencil clear; it is not the polygon-mode selector. Whether any shadow volumes or geometry actually draw between the two calls depends on the Scene/light/static/dynamic gates already mapped in [shadow passes](scene-shadow-stencil-apis.md). ## Wireframe pair

`Scene::RenderSinglePass` `0x1400d0f10` enters its late wireframe replay only when `g_nRenderWireframe != 0`. After temporarily clearing Scene `+0x140`, disabling/selecting states and setting white color, it calls `SetPolygonRenderMode(1)` at `0x1400d24ce`; it then replays draw buckets `0,1,7,8,2,3,5,6` under several color masks. After enabling the preceding states and setting black color, it calls `SetPolygonRenderMode(2)` at `0x1400d25c4` and restores the saved Scene `+0x140` value. This maps the paired state changes around a conditional replay, not extra geometry in every frame.
