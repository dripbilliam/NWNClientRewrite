## Standard movement

`moveParticleStandard` first requires positive live count `+0x190` and skips each particle whose byte `+0x70` is already set (`0x140068023`, `0x14006817b`). For active particles, source bit `6` transforms position and velocity from the prior emitter pose to the current pose. Source bits `8` and `10` optionally incorporate owner Gob or emitter displacement. It advances particle position `+0x10..+0x18` from velocity `+0x58..+0x60`, acceleration `+0x24..+0x2c`, particle scalar `+0x20` and delta (`0x140068313..0x140068390`). Source bit `2` calls `Scene::GetWindVector` `0x1400c9b90` at `0x1400683b4`; it adds the result to position. It then applies gravity from instance `mass+0xb0` and resets shared `CHitInfo` at `0x140068413`. These operations are CPU vector/quaternion math and state writes. Evidence `ev_7c630023378e591cda230f87e596ba613221990de48110da8fdb2128af6f4250`.

Under source bit `4` (`bounce`), the standard path builds old/new segment endpoints. With nonempty `chunkName`, it includes the particle's attached Gob in the `CHitInfo` skip list via `CExoArrayList<CAurObject*>::Add` `0x140060f30` at `0x14006846c`. It calls current Scene virtual `+0x58=Scene::HitCheck` `0x1400c9c90` at `0x1400684ba`. On a hit, `bounce_co+0x78` controls reflection/velocity; sufficiently low resulting speed sets particle byte `+0x70=1` at `0x1400685f3`, aligns orientation to the hit normal through `RotationArc` `0x14022e1c0` at `0x140068634`, and adjusts position/direction. It finally refreshes saved owner/emitter transform state for inherit flags. The hit test itself enters CPU BSP/Gob/Part geometry work in [Scene hit checks](lens-flare-hitcheck.md); its runtime object population and result are not statically fixed. Evidence movement above, `CHitInfo::ClearOutput` `ev_391efaa2c71d77c50629c5ae1383f738d6bafe72a96b8bfa6df3240054d1bfb5`, `Scene::HitCheck` `ev_23a159429e58b2516a0831fb360bcd81c2c01cef0b34b792df4ec5303d682d66`.

## Point-to-point gravity movement

`moveParticleP2PGrav` requires a positive live list, applies source bit `6` emitter-pose conversion and relevant inherit flags, then calculates a candidate position and optional bit-`2` wind sample at `0x1400678b8`. It reads the callback-selected target Gob's virtual `+0xc0=GetPosition` at `0x1400678db`. A target-distance test at `0x1400678e1..0x14006794c` compares the squared distance with instance `threshold+0xd8` squared; the close-enough arm sets the current particle age `+0x74` to lifespan `+0xac` at `0x140067f73..0x140067f7a` and exits this callback. Otherwise the path uses instance `drag+0x98` in a scalar exponent helper at `0x14006795f`, `grav+0xa8` with vector normalization `0x140230920` at `0x1400679a9`, then additional normalized/projection math, including `segmentproject` `0x140231aa0` at `0x140067aa3`. It writes velocity/position and may set age to lifespan on another target-proximity condition at `0x140067b0f`. It applies mass gravity and resets hit info at `0x140067bbb`. Evidence movement `ev_b89e0d72211d9d4e7768aea5eb40b92047d0ba7f139060c126976ba339a67256`, `normalize` `ev_7dd6b588c6e49eb4d0c5676491c6f5cdc4bb0020de3094e38777a8a7490ad4b3`, `segmentproject` `ev_d46899a8deae37157e0c91e7f9762ea1abf3d7fc58a3b5f06cad591fc742e6a7`.

Bit `4` then admits the same attached-Gob skip-list and `Scene::HitCheck` call at `0x140067c24/0x140067cf0`, followed by reflection/low-speed orientation work; low speed sets byte `+0x70` at `0x140067e20`. The P2P gravity loop does not test that byte at its entry, unlike Standard, so the flag alone is not its early skip gate. The callback does not call GL; it updates CPU particle and hit records and reads Scene/target Gob state. Evidence movement above.

## Point-to-point Bezier movement

`moveParticleP2PBezier` also requires a positive live list and applies optional source bit `6` emitter-pose conversion and bit-`2` wind at `0x140066bd2`. It reads target Gob virtual `+0xc0=GetPosition` at `0x140066bf2` and `+0xc8=GetOrientation` at `0x140066c92`. Particle age `+0x74` divided by lifespan `+0xac`, capped at `1`, forms cubic Bezier weights (`0x140066bf8..0x140066c8a`). Authored `p2p_bezier2+0xb4` and `p2p_bezier3+0xb8`, target orientation, emitter pose, and `combinetime+0x94` through scalar exponent helper `0x140c7c810` at `0x140066d32` shape its CPU candidate position. It writes particle position `+0x10..+0x18` at `0x140066ecb..0x140066edb`, applies mass gravity/drag, and clears hit info at `0x140066f1f`. Evidence movement `ev_b8b1a4445d7a5d341b2fa52cb291ba35f6da9c9265fd6978da973a9f9d0696e7`, scalar math `ev_95ec92e5fb72b50cb738eb3b867b3d0f6690b937bdc0f86b24590b2cfc4f238b`.

Source bit `4` admits attached-Gob skip-list setup and Scene hit test at `0x140066f7e/0x140067054`; a hit reflects motion, may set stop byte `+0x70` at `0x140067183`, and adjusts direction/orientation. This P2P loop also has no entry test of `+0x70`, so the stop byte alone does not suppress later Bezier updates. The actual target Gob subtype, target motion, controller values, collision result and attached model remain data-dependent. Evidence movement above.

## Shared CPU boundary and render handoff

`Scene::GetWindVector` forwards at `0x1400c9bb8` to `WindManager::GetGlobalWind` `0x1400f1560`, which samples two wind grids and point-source winds; those CPU descendants and grid production are in [scene animation/emitter](animation-and-emitter.md). `Scene::HitCheck` starts with `BSPHitCheck` `0x140229710` at `0x1400c9d22`, then conditionally tests Gob/Part geometry as mapped in [Scene hit checks](lens-flare-hitcheck.md). `CHitInfo::ClearOutput`, `normalize`, `segmentproject`, quaternion/vector helpers and scalar exponent math are CPU leaves here. None of the three movement callback bodies directly submits particle geometry or issues GL. A later eligible Scene emitter render callback `+0x1b0` at `0x1400c7dae` may route the updated particle data through `RenderEmitter` and conditional `glDrawArrays`; attached model Gobs take their own Scene path. Runtime callback selection, target discovery, flags/controllers, live list, Scene contents, material/shader gates and actual GL execution remain open.
