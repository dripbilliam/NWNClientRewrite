# Original per-frame BSP and fade-bucket maintenance

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256: `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. `Scene::Render` reaches `ManageSceneBSP` `0x1400db320` when the Scene has a BSP. This is a static control-flow map; it does not quantify how often a conditional branch runs in Arelith.

`ManageSceneBSP` returns from most work when Scene BSP `+0x188` is null. Otherwise it resets prior node fade and cached bucket counts. A camera-position delta over `0.3`, or a changed Scene sequence, refreshes its visibility origin; the motion/sequence decision also participates in dynamic Part and Gob relocation. It clears Gob in-volume bits, calls `FUN_1400d88b0` for camera follow/fade plane setup at `0x1400db560`, sizes texture-keyed mesh/fade bucket arrays to at least `max(5000, AurTextureGetMaxTexID()+1)`, and resets per-pass bucket counters. Both bucket array allocations are conditional capacity-growth branches, at `0x1400db5f9/0x1400db66f`. REA `ev_3e8c979129e4069878975c423d7b4b3a20b360b7445a01bb16bd51221532027d`.

LightManager virtual `+0x30=UpdateLights` at `0x1400db72d` and `+0x38=SetupLightsForThisPositionAndRadius` at `0x1400db760` refresh light positions and selection. With full dynamic lighting off, `DoStaticLightBuckets` at `0x1400db76f` processes queued static-light nodes, and a queued Part loop calls `SceneBSPUpdatePart` at `0x1400db78e`. The moving Part loop calls Part `+0x18=GetWorld` at `0x1400db808` and calls `SceneBSPUpdatePart` at `0x1400db864` only when the origin was invalidated or the world position changed. The Gob loop similarly compares current/previous positions or origin invalidation, fits the new sphere to a BSP node, removes it from the former node through `BSPSearch`/list removal, and inserts it into the new node, with conditional list reallocation. These are CPU scene placement and visibility preparation; their nested BSP/callback children are separate.

`BSPTraverseVolumeApprox` at `0x1400dba72` receives callback `FUN_1400dc140` to gather visible mesh, Gob, emitter, shadow, beam and bin candidates. `GrassIsEnabled` plus `HasBins` admits `BSPTraverse`/`CollectBins` at `0x1400dbabb`, then `AurProcessActiveBins` at `0x1400dbac0`. LightManager `+0x8=PrioritizeLights` at `0x1400dbad6` ranks the selected lights. The visible Gob list uses virtual `+0x2b8=IsInViewVolume` at `0x1400dbb0e`, with capacity growth only if needed. Camera Gob subobject `+0x2a8=EnableInViewVolume` at `0x1400dbb78` marks the current camera visible, then both fading bucket handlers run at `0x1400dbb7e/0x1400dbb83`. The callback, bin and light selection subtrees have their own [geometry](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md), [light selection](scene-light-selection.md) and [auxiliary pass](scene-auxiliary-passes.md) maps. The previous three site-free `ManageSceneBSP` ledger edges are replaced by exact physical callsites.

`HandleFadingInBucket` `0x1400d9670` and `HandleFadingOutBucket` `0x1400d9ab0` each require a nonempty corresponding node bucket.
