# NWN-75: Gob LOD resource switch to scene draw

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256: `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. Addresses are PE virtual addresses. This supplement connects the conditional `Gob::SetLODModel` switch to the later Scene bucket and GL route. Ghidra pseudocode is static evidence; it does not identify an active authored model, frame frequency or GPU cost. The broader [Gob/animation map](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md), [visible BSP callback](scene-bsp-visible-callback.md) and [ordinary TriMesh draw](part-trimesh-draw.md) hold their other descendants.

## Resource ownership before the switch

`NewCAurObject` `0x1400931f0` constructs the Gob. With `g_bEnableLOD` enabled and no caller-supplied preloaded model, it asks `CExoResMan::GetResObject` for resource type `0x81e` at `0x140093413`, creates and registers one when absent at `0x140093467`, and calls `CRes::Demand` at `0x14009346f`. A null demand result falls back to `FindModel` on the requested name at `0x14009357f`. A nonnull result is copied and parsed with `sscanf("%63s\n%f%63s\n%f%63s",...)` at `0x14009353f`, producing up to three model names and two thresholds. `FindModel` runs at `0x140093548/0x140093554/0x140093560` for those names, then the resource is released. With LOD disabled it calls `FindModel` directly at `0x1400933e4`; the caller-supplied preloaded model route calls `LoadModel` at `0x1400935d2`. `FindModel` searches the model cache and calls `IODispatcher::ReadSync` at `0x140098ecf` only on an admissible cache miss. These are construction-time conditional resource routes, not `SetLODModel` descendants. Evidence object `ev_8e0b947d4d92de1b3222644deb68df1e15d569e05500363ea10323cd930e956b`, model lookup `ev_d424beed21387f2bded08f967a7790602ec72f7d757a14273d1caefa230429fe`.

When a primary Model exists, `NewCAurObject` holds it at Gob `+0x68` and optional alternates at `+0x70/+0x78`. It calls `CreateInstanceTreeR` at `0x140093645/0x140093677/0x1400936a9`, storing their Part roots at Gob `+0x90/+0x98/+0xa0`; missing alternate Models leave corresponding roots absent. It initializes materials on present roots, stores thresholds at `+0x444/+0x448` and initial level zero at `+0x440`. This is why the later selection can swap prebuilt Model/Part pairs without an immediate model read or GPU call (`ev_8e0b947d4d92de1b3222644deb68df1e15d569e05500363ea10323cd930e956b`). Resource provider and authored subtype details beyond the named lookup are data dependent.

## BSP distance and LOD selection

On the BSP Scene route, `Scene::Render` `0x1400cc730` calls `ManageSceneBSP` `0x1400db320` at `0x1400ccb38`. The manager passes callback `0x1400dc140` to `BSPTraverseVolumeApprox` at `0x1400dba72`; the traversal invokes the callback at computed callsite `0x140229e1c` for visited nodes. For each payload Gob that survives sphere/plane tests, the callback invokes Gob `+0x2a8=EnableInViewVolume` at `0x1400dc2bd`, writes squared camera distance to Gob `+0x140`, and calls `Gob::SetLODModel` `0x140094b90` at `0x1400dc34f`. Rejected Gobs skip this call. Evidence traversal `ev_efc35458ae38284c80ef29426391194188ae68dcec4dc450bb62437b1e189ce1`, callback `ev_901383e0bdf24fe1d003abad9331df48ef421084ca94a06c5f73821df1e1e22b`.

`SetLODModel` returns if LOD is disabled, both alternate roots are absent, distance `+0x140` is negative, or the selected level equals `+0x440`. Otherwise it compares the squared distance with `(g_fLODScaleFactor × threshold)^2` for Gob `+0x444/+0x448`: near selects level 0, middle level 1, far level 2; a missing far root falls back to level 1, and a missing middle root falls back to level 0. The change branch permutes the active Model `+0x68` and root `+0x90` with the stored alternatives `+0x70/+0x78` and `+0x98/+0xa0`. Both `0↔1` and `0↔2` are pair swaps; `1↔2` rotates the three slots so each Model stays paired with its Part root. It writes new level `+0x440`, calls `Gob::Reset` at `0x140094d85`, and sets dirty byte `+0x1a5` at `0x140094d8a`. `Reset` rescales current animation runs against active Model `+0x68`, calls `AnimateHierarchy` on active Part root `+0x90` at `0x1400531b0`, and raises `GobReset` through virtual `+0x298` at `0x1400531d4`. The switch itself has no resource lookup or GL call. Evidence LOD `ev_24af28138424bd3c640690c296bec89d3a06a97eb6fc1b6be5bb3e24ad658edb`, reset `ev_56c1ecbfb13f67c652d55de7ff8316b9d6bb622c58350c1e6d781a730f09c898`. Callback target and side effects remain dependent on registered handlers.

## Active root into draw buckets

After traversal, `ManageSceneBSP` walks Scene Gob array `+0x130/+0x138`. Its virtual `+0x2b8=Gob::IsInViewVolume` call at `0x1400dbb0e` admits a Gob to `_gobbucket` at store `0x1400dbb50`; the original `Gob::IsInViewVolume` `0x140049110` reads flag `+0x1b`, which `EnableInViewVolume` writes. This is a separate post-traversal admission gate from the earlier LOD call (`ev_3e8c979129e4069878975c423d7b4b3a20b360b7445a01bb16bd51221532027d`, `ev_b6ff4fc3fa2d952a89a033a0b4fae0c999eef65c94ac91969bc38f30b37af631`, `ev_dee0be13ca7bb39bcc9a4578419f945c69a09eb52f15388d1b59dacb366eeae4`).

With a BSP root at Scene `+0x188`, `Scene::Render` later calls `AddPartsToDrawBuckets` `0x1400c38f0` at `0x1400ccdbe`. That function sorts `_gobbucket`, calls `Gob::ProcessGob` at `0x1400c426e`, and requires resulting Gob geometry byte `+0x1a4` plus positive visibility/fade `+0x108`. `ProcessGob` only acts when the LOD switch's dirty byte `+0x1a5` is set: it calls `TreeHasGeometry` at `0x140093b29`, stores `+0x1a4`, calls `TreeSetGeometryFlags` at `0x140093b3b` on the **new active root `+0x90`**, then clears dirty. The collector calls `AddPartsTreeToDrawBuckets` `0x1400c4620` with that active root at `0x1400c44ff` (`ev_6db915d06a51722ecdbc7a66e7a30f00cb61f018914bd5aa4d6d94ef6e419429`, `ev_37d96c0e1622ee82eaa535a5fca62328dd1d851f6df89a8ee797727b698e78ac`).

`AddPartsTreeToDrawBuckets` returns on a cleared Part geometry flag `+0x34`; otherwise it transforms the Part, tests virtual `+0x58=AsPartTriMesh`, and recurses through child Parts `+0x38/+0x40`. For a TriMesh it calls `IsPartRenderable` at `0x1400c46ef` (and again under shadow conditions at `0x1400c4835`), stages its model matrix, chooses a bucket from opacity/material/water/texture gates, and calls `DrawBucketManager::AddPartToBucket` at `0x1400c47a6/0x1400c4801/0x1400c4879`. A materialless mesh uses bucket 2 if opaque or 3 otherwise; material gates can choose buckets 4, 5 or 6 and an extra pass. `IsPartRenderable` requires Part byte `+0x9e`, a nonnull model node, its render field `+0x118`, and nonempty primitive data at `+0x284` or `+0x278`. These gates mean a selected LOD level need not produce a draw. Evidence collector `ev_7245333797a415fdd94c9cdf1b7512702927288fe071bae83702abdb27046e35`, renderability `ev_195f2606fe3da9fb947808e2b6a9ff86a62e9f2a1e7eba58ca1088af2473298d`.

## Draw boundary

`Scene::Render` calls its constructor-backed virtual `+0x210=RenderSinglePass` `0x1400d0f10` at `0x1400ccdc9` after bucket collection. Its virtual `+0x220=RenderDynamicGeometry` `0x1400ce110` at `0x1400d19e8` draws buckets 2 and 3 through `Scene::RenderDrawBucket` `0x1400cd210` at `0x1400ce298/0x1400ce2b6`. The other material buckets have separately gated calls in the [single-pass map](render-single-pass.md). `RenderDrawBucket` fetches admitted items and invokes the Part's virtual Draw slot `+0xc8` at `0x1400cd8d1`. For a constructor-backed TriMesh candidate this is `PartTriMesh::Draw` `0x1400a24c0`; its own active/model/primitive gates choose the ordinary `RenderFlat` `0x1400a4710` at `0x1400a261d` or conditional water/environment variants. `RenderFlat`, only with a VDO at primitive `+0x58`, calls `RenderInterface::RenderVertexData` at `0x1400a4815`; its thunk `0x1400beec0` reaches `GLRender::RenderVertexData` `0x1400b59b0`, which makes one conditional `glDrawElementsBaseVertex` extension call for indexed modes or imported `OPENGL32.DLL::glDrawArrays` at `0x1400b5bcb` for nonindexed mode. This is a complete conditional route from prebuilt LOD selection to a GL draw API, not a claim that any one model executes it in a recorded frame.
