# NWN-75: authored Light node to scene light selection

Pinned installed `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. This maps a Light model-node source through typed instance and scene membership to the already mapped [light selection](scene-light-selection.md), [shader uniform publication](shader-uniform-dirty-gates.md) and conditional [lens flare pass](scene-auxiliary-passes.md). Exact physical sites are in [the ledger](model-light-source-selection-ledger.json). It does not claim that an authored light or flare is active in a particular area.

## Text and binary source

Text `ParseNode` `0x1400a0d90` constructs `MdlNodeLight` `0x140087640` at `0x1400a0ecf` for a `light` node. The constructor sets kind `3`, initializes controller/flare lists, defaults light-priority source `+0xf8` to 5 and several source flags, and installs parse `+0x8`, postprocess `+0x10`, control `+0x18` and CreateInstance `+0x28=0x140092b10`. The parser `MdlNodeLight::InternalParseField` `0x14009db90` recognizes five controller families: `color`/key/bezier to Part offset `+0x68` at calls `0x14009dc3d/0x14009dc7b/0x14009dcb7`; `radius` to `+0x74` at `0x14009dd3b/0x14009dd79/0x14009ddb5`; `shadowradius` to `+0x7c` at `0x14009de33/0x14009de71/0x14009dead`; `verticaldisplacement` to `+0x80` at `0x14009df2b/0x14009df69/0x14009dfa5`; and `multiplier` to `+0xa8` at `0x14009e023/0x14009e061/0x14009e09d`. Each family chooses `AddGenericControllerSingle`, `AddGenericController`, or `AddBezierController` for its literal/key/bezier token. The parser checks token length below `0x400`, then delegates remaining base-node fields at `0x14009e606`. ## Part publication and frame consumers

`CreateInstanceTreeR` calls node `+0x28` in stored hierarchy order, selecting the constructor-installed `MdlNodeLightCreateInstance` `0x140092b10` for this subtype. The wrapper allocates a 0xc0-byte `PartLight`, points it at its source node, installs `PartLight` vtable and copies node shadow/dynamic/ambient flags `+0x108/+0x104/+0x100/+0xfc` to Part `+0x78/+0x88/+0x8c/+0x84`. For an eligible owner Gob it appends the PartLight pointer to Gob `+0x470` at `0x140092c5c` and can mark Gob `+0xf0`. It does not register that pointer in the Scene light manager by itself. A separately named `MdlNodeLight::InternalCreateInstance` `0x140091980` has corresponding logic, but the constructor-backed field here points to the wrapper `0x140092b10`; this map does not assume the separate body executes. REA wrapper `ev_4283fffe096b356096a968d4968ddba36fc1686d138919ef561a00ae65480310`, separate body `ev_25af76efbd6ac765f072dba4588818d9c3a615b06444c43a3d562b9bbcfd70b3`.

When Scene BSP membership admits the Part, `SceneBSPAddPart` `0x1400dcfd0` identifies `PartLight` through virtual `+0x48` at `0x1400dd00f`, fits its position/radius into BSP at `0x1400dd02d`, appends it to the node light list if absent, and invokes LightManager virtual `+0x20=AddLight` `0x14007dbd0` at `0x1400dd05d`. `AddLight` checks its general list, obtains Part world position through virtual `+0x18`, then adds the pointer to general `+0x10`, static `+0x30` if Part kind `+0x8c==0`, and dynamic/effect `+0x20` under the owner/kind flags. This is CPU list membership and pose publication; no GL call. REA membership `ev_7c167ceadc5b6fa383cf85d87daafa4cd1510a8d3ceec2e28e904cb53f9b9404`, AddLight `ev_7c1541dcaf89f1b66d254bba0bce15d8ac8760fc7b0b37d3b66231d30bdfcc14`, constructor-backed LightManager vtable in [light selection](scene-light-selection.md).

At animation control time, node field `+0x18=0x140092b00` tail-jumps to `MdlNodeLight::InternalControl` `0x140052480`; it calls the Part's `+0x48=AsPartLight` at `0x1400524a6` and tail-jumps to base `MdlNode::InternalControl` `0x140051c50`. The base loops controller records and blends float/vector/quaternion outputs into the Part at their recorded offsets. In the frame light path, `LightManager::UpdateLights`, `SetupLightsForThisPositionAndRadius`, sorting/fade and `SetLightGL` consume those Part fields and eventually stage cached GLRender light values. `GLRender::SetUniforms` conditionally publishes changed light generations through imported `glUniform*`; [light selection](scene-light-selection.md) and [uniform gates](shader-uniform-dirty-gates.md) ledger those descendants. `PartLight::Draw` `0x1400a2480` tail-dispatches to `PartLight::Mat` `0x1400a2f00`, which updates cached global color through `RenderInterface::SetColor4f`; it does no primitive submission. Its optional texture array instead feeds the gated lens flare path.
