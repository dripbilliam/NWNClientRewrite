## `CAurGUIImageInternal` owner and draw chain

## `CAurGUIStringInternal` owner and draw chain

`NewCAurGUIString` `0x1400703f0` allocates a 0x68-byte object through constructor `0x14006e410`. It owns a heap copy of the text at `+0x18`, optional font texture safe pointer at `+0x20`, color, line metadata and alignment state; destructor `0x14006e630` releases the font texture and text/line allocations. The factory has only DATA/exception-table incoming references in the exact query; `Draw` `0x14006f200` is in vtable `0x140d85260` at `+0x8`, and `DrawBoundingRect` `0x14006fc20` at `+0xf0`, both with DATA-only incoming references. Neither body is shown as a direct caller of the other. Thus each is a separately mapped virtual target, with no established live producer/caller. Evidence: REA `ev_0557a2435a658c972ba17ccbcc2cd97b1b29a12ad14cbd8eb237f99e3a148fad`, `ev_012dcb118b10e5536e93eab75f80b260ca5359d5ba579b883dc8df6b0e3f5e36`, `ev_6228707060866ffd4a1e01006fde15ea680eced0ed1d9dbf0cab6cf514edd57f`, `ev_76808902501418a84a6bd2173f2d79c350abaa6f762f60328bd4a90ee65382b7`, `ev_2f93f093b10644607dcf33b723801911e7a911c060faa530348fb575c8af344f`, `ev_1e0187ba056bd90a6d5909b617c4706d0b81ed5175923b8ada611010ce668972`.

`Draw()` returns when both object integers `+0x8` and `+0x10` are zero or when its font texture pointer is null. Otherwise it obtains font info, conditionally calls `CAurTexture::BuildAndStore`, sizes/allocates a shared vertex stream for the text, binds the font texture through virtual `+0x100`, calculates glyph coordinates and alignment/wrapping on CPU, writes six vertices per drawn glyph, commits the stream, and calls `RenderInterface::RenderVertexStreamData` `0x1400beed0` with primitive type 3. ## `CGuiPanel::AnimateScene` owner and callsites

`CGuiPanel::AnimateScene` `0x1401fc210` takes the panel-owned `CSceneView*` at `+0xb8`, dereferences its Scene and invokes Scene virtual `+0x20`. The original Scene table resolves that slot to `Scene::Animate` `0x1400c5210`. Two direct client callsites exist: `0x1406f8891` in `FUN_1406f8820` and `0x1406fc96c` in `FUN_1406fc900`. Each is inside owner `+0x374 != 0` and owner `+0x68 != 0` branches, passes the panel at owner `+0x58` with delta `1e+06`, then clears the flag at `+0x374`. This is a synchronous Scene/Gob/Part animation dispatch on that caller's thread, not an immediate GL draw. The mapped [Scene animation branch](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md) follows Gob/Part/event descendants; later GUI scene render can submit changed models through the common conditional GL path. Active owner state, Scene contents and later visibility remain runtime dependent. ## Open boundary

Indirect virtual/API consumers and active runtime objects remain unproven. Panel `AnimateScene` has two direct callers; its active owner state and Scene/Gob population remain conditional.
