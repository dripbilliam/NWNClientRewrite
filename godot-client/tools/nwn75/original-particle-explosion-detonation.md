## Update gate, CPU wind and burst

`Scene::DoEmitterBucket` calls `PartEmitter::AnimateParticles` at `0x1400c7c94` only when Scene animation or owner Gob force-animation admits the emitter. That method rejects fading owner/tile states, derives pose, caps delta at `0.5` and calls Explosion virtual `+0x100=Update` at `0x1400615bf`. Explosion Update `0x1400647c0` converts float birthrate `+0x74` to a signed integer with `CVTTSS2SI` at `0x1400647fc`. It obtains owner adjusted scale through a computed Gob virtual `+0x2d0` at `0x14006481e`, calls `chkParticleLife` at `0x14006482e` to age/recycle old particles, updates previous position in motion-blur mode `6`, advances live rotation, then calls its installed movement callback `+0x170` at `0x140064949`. These precede the burst test even when no detonation follows. The movement target is installed as standard or one of two point-to-point methods according to source flags; the active target is runtime-dependent. Evidence Scene `ev_b4881207528882fb849869474dcd3a31c18cf9f00dad89280e126d85fcdd7ed3`, AnimateParticles `ev_94298fdbef50a777edca20b8e740cf4bb6069376493f19ec2c974abe89f3eb53`, Explosion Update `ev_03636177ada4f8c1fadb060f39e2f123fc13ac3ea56976d89888adfc41516546`.

The explosion-specific decisions are separate. At `0x14006494e`, an **exact zero** converted rate skips wind and particle spawn. For nonzero rate, trigger byte `+0x108` must be set at `0x140064956`, and latch `+0x1c8` must be below `1.0` at `0x140064963..0x14006496b`. A nonzero `blastRadius` at source `+0xa4` admits the optional call to `WindManager::AddPointSourceWind` `0x1400f1250` at `0x1400649bc`, passing current emitter position, `blastRadius`, `blastLength` and `1.0`. The WindManager appends a 0x1c-byte point-source record under `+0x10/+0x18`, allocating 16 entries initially or doubling capacity through `CExoArrayList<PointSourceWind>::Allocate` at `0x1400f12a3`. This is a CPU state publication read later by the shared wind-sampling path in [animation and emitter](animation-and-emitter.md); it does not call GL. Evidence Explosion Update above, wind append `ev_55ce15660c7d7afb27793491e735d30b64081fc0b68566e0b27cb4b3cef8f5e6`, allocation helper `ev_bb002aa535cda3f32a2eba5fc7cef38bc3daccb6f11bb6e9f4492de74f8c2d94`.

Only after the wind branch does `0x1400649c1..0x1400649c3` require the signed converted rate to be **positive** for particles. Thus a negative nonzero converted rate can publish blast wind when its other gates pass while scheduling zero particles. Positive count is copied to loop counter at `0x1400649c9`; each iteration removes a pointer from reuse list `+0x130/+0x138`, or tests source `chunkName+0x158` at `0x140064a2d` and constructs Chunky at `0x140064a57` or ordinary Particle at `0x140064a85`. It calls particle virtual `+0x10=initialize` at `0x140064a9e`, with constructor-backed fresh targets `Particle::initialize` `0x140066450` and `ChunkyParticle::initialize` `0x1400663d0`; a reused pointer's subtype remains runtime data. Base initialize can reach random-position, inherited-velocity and random-direction CPU helpers and CRT RNG. Chunky initialize calls base initialize, reattaches its model Gob when present and requests `impact`. The update appends to live list `+0x188/+0x190` through `CExoArrayList<Particle*>::Add` at `0x140064aab`, then calls `chkRandom` at `0x140064ac6` for optional authored atlas frame selection. These initializer and reuse descendants are shared with Fountain and are covered in [particle lifecycle](original-particle-effect-lifecycle.md). Evidence Explosion Update above, Particle init `ev_6e072379e7c8ab3444fd75c3c2427a822035b68918abb247d9747090214e118c`, Chunky init `ev_65645e3fdedf1bc0e299e49d0f38911da109e38bf8004db95233c840fd213747`, live-list helper `ev_2a3390eff427634ecff451dd4cde5a9540a4f3858f8ee29aeb00ea5385185349`, atlas helper `ev_0d319e3bfd443ed59db1b8f7bc74500510aa112b058457cd319f65fa205f0dba`.

At Update exit, it sets latch `+0x1c8` to `1.0` if trigger remains set, otherwise clears it to `0.0` (`0x140064ad5..0x140064ae9`), then always clears trigger `+0x108` at `0x140064af1`. A trigger received while latch is already `1.0` does not spawn again; an intervening Update with no new trigger clears the latch. The trigger is an event flag, not an accumulating particle timer. Evidence Explosion Update above.

## Conditional presentation and open boundary

The Scene emitter bucket later sorts eligible positive-live emitters and calls installed render callback `+0x1b0` at `0x1400c7dae`. With empty `chunkName`, Standard/Linked/MotionBlurred callbacks enter shared `RenderEmitter` `0x140062900`, which commits a particle vertex stream at `0x140063ff5` and, under fade/material/shader gates, calls `RenderInterface::RenderVertexStreamData` at `0x140064109`. Its thunk reaches `GLRender::RenderVertexStreamData`; the nonindexed stream can call imported `glDrawArrays` at `0x1400b5d66`. With nonempty `chunkName`, `RenderNothing` updates attached model Gobs rather than issuing a particle quad draw; those Gobs have their own Scene draw gates. Exact shared modes and GL leaves are in [emitter render callbacks](original-particle-render-callbacks-fountain.md). Explosion Update itself has no GL call. Evidence shared renderer `ev_34f8fbdda88642e9a5fcc853ff16fcb539af3e4923e97fbbca235e05c24b6605`, GL body `ev_faed7a96ea930f48e2170d7800f7dc1d4110c2b5267b35fe6813a21dee29899a`, import name `ev_92d60e87df74fc97359537fb3c4ba78843de0d7593ef2799396051c062130f98`.

Live `Detonate` events, current animation/controller values, owner Gob, scene/wind manager, blast fields, reuse population, model resource success, movement callback and shader/GL admission are runtime or authored-data frontiers. The static branch establishes possible work and exact conditional edges, not their frequency.
