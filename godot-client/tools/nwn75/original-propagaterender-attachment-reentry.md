## Render reentry and concrete targets

`PropagateRender` `0x140056d80` first repeats a case-insensitive `propagaterender` check. It reads the stored attached Gob context at `+0x450`; if a behavior is installed, it calls that behavior's virtual `+0x18=Control(0)` at `0x140056dae`. The five constructor-backed Attach vtables put base `Control` `0x140054cb0`, Translate `0x140055010`, Rotate `0x140054f40`, NoOrientation `0x140054ed0`, and GUIScale `0x140054e30` in this slot. The derived controls call the base and update attached pose/scale according to their mode. The active behavior pointer can change, so these five are the bounded family for the observed Attach construction route, not a guarantee for every receiver at callback time. The callback then **tail-jumps** through attached Gob virtual `+0x20=Render(true)` at `0x140056dbe`, regardless of whether a behavior was present. With the three observed constructor-backed receiver vtables, this selects `Gob::Render`, `StringGob::Render`, or Camera's gated tail jump to `Gob::Render`. The new `Render(true)` may draw a Part tree or text and then raise `propagaterender` on that child, which can invoke another Attach callback. No fixed attachment-depth cap appears in `PropagateRender` or `Gob::DoEvent`; the actual chain, ownership and visibility gates are runtime/asset-dependent. `Gob::PartDraw` and StringGob text descendants reach their existing conditional GL paths, as mapped in [normal Gob draw](scene-no-bsp-sky-gob.md) and [GUI text presentation](gui-presentation.md). This is a render reentry route, not proof of recursion in any particular live scene.
