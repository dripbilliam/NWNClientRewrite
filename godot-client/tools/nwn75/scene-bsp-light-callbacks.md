# NWN-75: original BSP light callbacks and grass color rebuild

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. These are conditional descendants of the Scene/BSP render path. The direct calls below are recorded by exact site in `branch-ledger.json`; the graph remains partial.

## Two entry gates

When `g_bFullDynamicLighting` changes, `Scene::Render` `0x1400cc730` traverses the BSP with `LightNodeMeshes` `0x1400da420` and then `LightBins` `0x1400da3d0` as callbacks at `0x1400ccce2` and `0x1400cccf8`. This is a lighting-mode transition, not the unconditional frame path. `LightNodeMeshes` checks each node's triangle-mesh list, with a single AABB mesh fallback when the list is empty. For each eligible mesh with faces, it computes world position, scans candidate LightManager lights, filters light state/color and sphere overlap, grows a temporary light list if needed, then calls `LightPartTriMesh` `0x1400da970` at `0x1400da64f`. It releases that temporary list at `0x1400da680` or `0x1400da688`. REA `ev_01f7ebf8f60e70f06e02c80c2a7898948faf203cfddd389e3c2f8271d3820e6a`.

In non-full-dynamic mode, `ManageSceneBSP` calls `DoStaticLightBuckets` `0x1400d9330` at `0x1400db76f`. With a nonempty queued `_staticlightbucket`, it calls `LightNodeTile` `0x1400da6c0` for each queued BSP node at `0x1400d941e`; if the BSP exists it also calls `BSPTraverse` `0x140229c60` at `0x1400d944d`, passing `LightBins` at `0x1400d9446`. The routine clears the queue count after processing. `LightNodeTile` scans the node tile's Parts, admits triangle meshes through virtual Part `+0x58` at `0x1400da75d`, skips meshes without faces, selects nearby lights, and calls the same `LightPartTriMesh` at `0x1400da8ff`. REA `ev_4899bbd0cdf5da2fd20a8a534a41e0a8768e17b14ce98c2c2a17e18d5a433d2f`, `ev_d2975d94bd910b37563c6a42c900cc71f6f2b8ac76d13a40b2ce3de88f0dab67`. Its virtual admission target is the previously resolved `PartTriMesh::AsPartTriMesh`; the REA call destination remains computed at that site.

`LightPartTriMesh`'s color and buffer update descendants are mapped in [geometry and draw boundary](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md). Both callbacks only prepare/select work in their own bodies; they do not issue a direct draw or GL call. Their vector/math, temporary-allocation and release callsites are site-backed in the ledger.

## Bin color and grass data

`LightBins` `0x1400da3d0` calls `CAurTriangleBin::ReloadColor` `0x14006d950` for each bin at `0x1400da3fb` (REA `ev_7d04430520480b9179afa1caaa3d3583f717f714362c36a9cd40f01710b112d9`). For a triangle list with an existing vertex-data item, `ReloadColor` calls `VertexDataObject::RemoveItem` `0x1400be140` at `0x14006d994`, clears that item reference, then calls `CAurTriangleList::BuildGrassPolys` `0x14006cb80` at `0x14006d9a5` (REA `ev_6484b42d9a72173fe89344b43ce19e90f016e2b063cfabd40ecdaf01e86405eb`). `RemoveItem` subtracts and zeros the item vertex/index counts; its body has no callee or direct GL API (REA `ev_21c005643577f7234593107ba3a5a8416c477bf4be94a75667d1cbbc6462dece`).

`BuildGrassPolys` requires a positive grass count and no existing item. It obtains a VDO token at `0x14006cbf3`, adds an item at `0x14006cc0a`, calls `CreateTriangleGrass` `0x14006cd70` for each triangle with positive grass count at `0x14006ccf0`, then commits static and dynamic item data at `0x14006cd19` and `0x14006cd2b`. Its `GetTickCount` call at `0x14006cd30` is an imported timer boundary; `FUN_140c7ed2c` at `0x14006cd38` updates the RNG state. REA `ev_23e6ab673118fa2e284fb626ba6f36909607b8d3fb1637131624cc038c74e186`.

`CreateTriangleGrass` seeds the original RNG from triangle coordinates, samples barycentric positions/orientations, conditionally locks the source pool to interpolate vertex color, writes six vertices per grass blade into the VDO item data, and unlocks the pool afterward. It has no direct draw. Its 19 direct helper callsites, including RNG, vector math, item-data access and pool lock/unlock, are recorded in the ledger (REA `ev_3a776ae1c4360a673a16abc5089f8a66f92cba02b6ff84a0e88ce3fd2af21b5d`). VDO commit and `UnlockPool` descendants are mapped in [primitive/VDO preparation](primitive-vdo-preparation.md). This branch may allocate and publish GPU buffers on an invalidation; the callback entry conditions do not establish that it runs on every ordinary frame.
