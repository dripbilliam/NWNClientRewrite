## Source choice and resource demand

`AurGetImageWrapper` `0x1400e5c60` tries the installed `CAuroraInterface::GetCompressedRaster` `0x1400f56c0` only when its compression argument is nonzero and global `nocompression` is zero. On null it tries the installed 16-bit and raster callbacks. `GetCompressedRaster` calls `CExoResMan::Exists` four times for KTX resource type `0x819`, DDS `0x7f1`, 4PC `0x80b` and TGA `3` at `0x1400f5738/0x1400f575c/0x1400f5780/0x1400f57a4`. Its selection compares returned storage classes in order 2, 5, 3 and 1; a 4PC/TGA resource at the considered class can leave compressed selection to the later fallback, while KTX precedes DDS at the same class. It constructs `CAuroraCompressedTextureDDS` at `0x1400f5892` or `CAuroraCompressedTextureKTX` at `0x1400f58c7`. The constructors obtain or register a `CResDDS`/`CResKTX` under the chosen type, reusing a resource object when found. The two constructor-backed wrapper vtables are `0x140d91de0` (DDS) and `0x140d91f00` (KTX). Their slot `+0x18` is `LoadCompressedTexture` `0x1400f3100` or `0x1400f3760`; `GetCompressedRaster` invokes that slot at `0x1400f58da`. ## Header fields, format and later GL boundary

`CResDDS::OnResourceServiced` `0x1400f9d10` tests the first word for `DDS `; that arm places the data pointer at resource base `+0x80`, while the other arm treats the first `0x14` bytes as the alternate compressed header. `CResKTX::OnResourceServiced` `0x1400f9ff0` stores the header pointer and places data after `0x40` bytes plus the header's key-value length at `+0x3c`. These callbacks set loaded fields and do not themselves draw or upload. The observed bodies do not establish full length/header validation. Constructor-backed `CResDDS`/`CResKTX` vtables `0x140d92a70/0x140d92aa0` place these service callbacks at `+0x20`, reached through successful resource service. Evidence service bodies `ev_097c2f0c84209ea04b2c0b9a2e7dc8ac38795f154fe68729e9f66cbfa179e123`, `ev_4150ca3023a73e938bedf81721706d21fa165bc31282dd87cfda238916bd2b51`; vtable bytes `ev_c9c040dc572871846a436f95250a85df147948f9cfba0301bc5295b792035a99`, `ev_bc1ba902a2fb2631cd54d8c38e0c12f030d16ed48405e65e2ba477147950bfa7`.

After a successful load, `GetCompressedRaster` calls wrapper slots `+0x8` (attributes) at `0x1400f5937`, `+0x28` (container format) at `0x1400f5940`, and `+0x10` (data pointer) at `0x1400f5979`. DDS targets are `0x1400f30a0/0x1400f30e0/0x1400f30c0`; KTX targets are `0x1400f3710/0x1400f3750/0x1400f3730`. DDS attributes read either the alternate header or DDS width/height and FourCC, mapping DXT/ATI formats to internal channel/type codes and block-compressed byte count. KTX attributes read width/height and selected ETC2 internal formats from the header, mapping to two internal codes and a calculated block size. DDS container format returns 1 for the alternate header or 2 for `DDS `; KTX returns 3. The loaded flag gates both attribute and data access. Evidence DDS attributes `ev_6f6a2cb3d0b57b5f86a8d97fd85bdc68ebdc8da8b9bf18b25bb921be0fd6bfa2`, KTX attributes `ev_9ff4c788a256eae0e07cfdeb9b89721cf22febf708e1c9a974581b46bc62bd1c`, DDS format `ev_38a8bb3c2598e19742fddfea2305cd02626e0e28384d740a4a9f8c3fadf3a70b`.

`AurGetImageWrapper` maps the returned container/format codes to its image fields. A later eligible `CAurTextureBasic::BuildAndStoreTexture` → `glImageBasic` path selects `RenderInterface::CreateTexture` → `GLRender::CreateTexture` `0x1400af440`, whose compressed branches call the loaded `glCompressedTexImage2D` pointer at `0x1400af745` or `0x1400af800`. Material binding and actual Part draw are separate gated descendants in [texture consumer binding](texture-consumer-binding.md) and [geometry draw](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md). The upload shape is mapped in [texture publication](texture-publication-per-frame.md); this source callback does not call GL. Runtime format, mip/tile selection and GL driver behavior remain open.

## Release and frontier

`CAurTextureBasic::FreeImage` `0x1400eb640` calls installed `CAuroraInterface::ReleaseRaster` `0x1400f74d0` when callback-owned raster data is present. For compressed data, `ReleaseRaster` chooses a prefix offset from container format 1/2/3, checks the stamped DDS/KTX type, recovers the wrapper pointer, invokes its vtable slot `+0x20=UnloadCompressedTexture` at `0x1400f7548`, then slot zero deleting destructor with flag 1 at `0x1400f755b`. DDS/KTX unload methods `0x1400f32a0/0x1400f3900` call `CRes::Release` when loaded; the deleting destructors also release a still-loaded resource and destroy helper ownership. An unknown prefix/type logs and returns. This is resource lifetime after image use, not a second GPU upload. Open boundaries are active texture source and cache state, malformed or unsupported DDS/KTX header behavior, other compressed-resource callers, RTTI-stripped or external texture subclasses, and the later material/visibility/driver gates. The static route proves possible request, upload and release paths but not their execution frequency.
