## Entry and codec setup

## Packet decode and PCM publication

`UpdateMovie` returns before reading a packet when end `internal+0x28` is set, pause byte `+0x19` is set, a time gate delays the non-direct route, or codec block `+0x20` is null. Otherwise it calls `nestegg_read_packet` at `0x14023432e`, gets track and timestamp at `0x140234343/0x140234356`, and enters audio handling only when byte `internal+0x18` is zero and packet track equals the saved audio track `block+0x344`. `StepSingleFrame` `0x1402341b0` and `Seek` `0x140233aa0` temporarily set both bytes `+0x18/+0x19` to one around their recursive frame-advance calls, supporting the audio-suppression interpretation of `+0x18`; `PauseMovie`/`UnpauseMovie`/`PauseToggle` operate on `+0x19` without a direct OpenAL pause call. On the audio branch's first packet while block `+0xf0` is zero, the updater obtains three track codec-data records through `nestegg_track_codec_data` at `0x1402346cc/0x140234718/0x140234760` and submits each to `vorbis_synthesis_headerin` at `0x1402346f9/0x140234741/0x140234789`. ## Completion and teardown

When `nestegg_read_packet` returns less than one, the updater sets end flag `internal+0x28`, except the loop/seek branch that resets clock fields and continues. The direct movie caller's zero result can trigger movie object destruction in the client completion route; Nui lifetime depends on widget ownership. The movie internal destructor calls `ShutDown` `0x140233f50` at `0x140232ee7`. Under initialized-object `internal+0x8` and nonnull codec-block `+0x20` gates, `ShutDown` calls imported `alSourceStop` at `0x14023404e`, detaches the source with `alSourcei` at `0x140234062`, queries `0x1016` and unqueues buffers at `0x140234078/0x1402340a0`, then calls `alDeleteSources` at `0x1402340c0` and `alDeleteBuffers(0x3c)` at `0x1402340d2`. It frees the PCM/video output buffer, conditionally clears Vorbis block and DSP when block `+0xf0` is nonzero, always clears Vorbis comment/info, frees the codec block and deletes the movie texture. This is the mapped movie-owned OpenAL/Vorbis teardown to imported API and local library routines; the distinct VP8 worker destructor frontier is detailed in the [video publication supplement](original-movie-decode-frame-publication.md).
