# NWN-75: dynamic-shadow shader parameter publication

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. This is a static continuation of the [shadow pass](shadows-and-dynamic.md), not a measured frame-cost claim. The direct connectors below are in `scene-dynamic-shadow-shader-params-ledger.json`.

`Scene::RenderDynamicShadows` `0x1400ce3a0` is reached from the enabled shadow pass and may also flush a full projection element stream. It commits that stream before its cached shader test. If the cached `vs_shadowvol`/`fs_shadowvol` program at `0x14140af88` is null, it calls `RenderInterface::GetCustomShaderProgram` at `0x1400ce4a6`; a null result exits before any item loop. The selected PartLight at Scene `+0x50` and whether it is the active dynamic projection light choose bucket `13,14` or `13,11,14,12`. The exact shadow selection and geometry gates remain in the linked shadow map. REA body `ev_620616fe6c072ac0b0830026a6636ae1d5c48a2937ca89cbd35a0e00f65769fb`.

For a nonnull bucket item, the virtual `+0x58=AsPartTriMesh` result, its primitive/VDO, and positive pending element count gate the draw body. Only then the routine stages the cached shadow program at `0x1400ce73e`, copies four light-derived values, and calls `RenderInterface::SetShaderParamv4(0, value)` at `0x1400ce752`. For a light radius at most 1000, the four values come from the selected PartLight position/radius fields; above 1000, the body uses a normalized direction and fourth component `-1`. It then calls `SetShaderParamf(1, value)` at `0x1400ce786`. With no Part projection pointer at `+0xa0`, that float is `-20`; otherwise it is `min(projection+0x60, 0)-0.1`. `RenderVertexData` follows at `0x1400ce7aa`, and the routine clears that primitive's pending element count afterward. These calls therefore belong to an admitted item, not every shadow-pass entry. The vector interface copies sixteen bytes and directly calls `GLRender::SetShaderParamv4` `0x1400b7cb0` at `0x1400bf291`; the float interface is a tail jump to `GLRender::SetShaderParamf` `0x1400b7c70`. Both GLRender setters **unconditionally** increment generation `0x1413d273c` and store their supplied value, without a changed-value comparison. Their bodies do not call GL. Evidence: wrappers `ev_d84943450be4be69b541fcc5818933bdfb69aadb34ce9be2f3cec8aea7bd19d5`, `ev_2523d0d35f40d496e1265858c5d98f562b6877583848b634b9ffee9aaa0c0ba2`; setters `ev_94fbe4a0e8139fb62ce8e9ceb54ff388d0e69c0efff0f6c0482505b9ddd7e065`, `ev_6e69c5d5227ece77bf9161896a919c5217d839d5d2d7dfb895900cd2ba82b26d`; direct wrapper sites `ev_f0b8f9b895744afab3b6096f510e898d1e7ef01199d10868b0dd09881c04802a`, `ev_2f0f7a8da275c36c5364757839c041b1324a3a4dccb41069e4da59aec79db944`.

The [ordinary VDO draw](part-trimesh-draw.md) calls `GLRender::SetProgram` at `0x1400b5ac0`; its two branches tail-jump to `GLRender::SetUniforms` `0x1400b8380` at `0x1400b7a85/0x1400b7aff`, before the conditional indexed/array GL draw. `SetUniforms` reads generation `0x1413d273c` at `0x1400ba26a` and compares it with the selected program's cached `+0x4c`. A difference enters the shader-constant group. Within that group, location `+0x1d34` (`projectionSource`) and location `+0x1d38` (`projectionWorldZClip`) each need a valid location **and** a difference from the program's cached vector/float before `glUniform4fv` at `0x1400ba327` or `glUniform1f` at `0x1400ba361`. The two unconditional setter increments can make this group run for an admitted shadow item even if a value repeats; they do not imply two uploads or any GL upload. The same group also checks other constants, including `particleSoftness`; their values are not attributed to this shadow item.
