## Timer, volume and list gates

`Render` returns immediately when its `+0x30` field is null. Otherwise it reads `CExoTimers::GetMillisecondTimer` at `0x1401e08f6`. A nonzero `+0xb8` fade-in duration, or a nonzero `+0xb4` fade-out duration, uses elapsed milliseconds and clamps the interpolation to `[0,1]`. Each changed master-volume component at `+0xac/+0xa8/+0xa4` calls `ApplyNewVolume` (six callsites spanning `0x1401e09a5..0x1401e0bd9`). On fade-out completion it can stop and remove eligible streaming sources. `ApplyNewVolume` walks the streaming and ordinary playing lists and calls their subtype `SetVolume` only for nonnull entries. The streaming setter requires an initialized sound device and nonzero source ID before imported `alSourcef` at `0x1401e1bb9`; the ordinary setter additionally selects 2D/3D voice and category gain before its imported `alSourcef` at `0x1401e1a97`. Their error checks and log calls are conditional. `Render` separately walks delayed one-shots, active streams, playing sources and nonplaying loops. A delayed source crosses its millisecond delay before `CExoSoundSourceInternal::Play` at `0x1401e0c8b`; the nonplaying-loop pass calls the same body at `0x1401e10dc`. Streams have status, fade and pause gates before `Stop`, `SetVolume` or `Pause`. `Pause` queries imported `alGetSourcei` and only calls `alSourcePause` when the state is `0x1012`. `Stop` calls `CExoSoundStreamThread::StopStream`; that helper enters its critical section, conditionally calls `alSourceStop` and `alDeleteSources`, removes the stream from its list, then leaves the critical section. This establishes synchronization around stop, not the thread that invoked `Render` or the sound worker's whole execution. For playing sources, `Render` asks OpenAL for source state at `0x1401e0f87/0x1401e0fb7`. It may remove and shut down a source when its sound owner is inactive, no usable voice is present, OpenAL state is not playing, or `GetWithinRangeOfListener` rejects it. The range helper returns true for 2D sources; for 3D it computes listener/source vector magnitude and compares the source range, a CPU math boundary. `ShutDownSource` removes the source from its current list, then, under the initialized-device and nonzero-voice gates, calls imported `alSourceStop`, `alSourcei`, `alDeleteSources` and `alDeleteBuffers` before releasing a demanded resource. These are conditional source transitions, not one complete stop/delete sequence on every render. ## Voice creation and API boundary

`CExoSoundSourceInternal::Play` `0x1401df2e0` requires a usable sound owner, may demand a WAV resource, checks listener range and priority/voice availability, and dispatches to `PlaySourceOn2DVoice` `0x1401dfad0` or `PlaySourceOn3DVoice` `0x1401e0050`. Both obtain resource size/data, try `OpenAL_SDL_LoadWAV_RW`, and fall back to `CResWave::GetWaveInfo` for a supported PCM format. On successful sound data and a free voice they generate and fill a buffer, generate a source, attach it, set properties/volume and call imported `alSourcePlay`. The 3D branch additionally sets range and position with `alSourcef`/`alSource3f`. The 2D/3D variants and their exact import callsites are in the ledger. `Play` also has direct OpenAL stop/play calls on its existing-voice path. REA Play `ev_25428e99d5f3cf2376ec704157a1e8f8f2f5301676678fbc989b49bc0cc5eb15`, 2D `ev_5524593f25af2bcd3b1fcafee6da2b31cb582bcfa53c03a44b17f6e1ce7c7fc6`, 3D `ev_21bc4503e9932c744a870785da47fea72b21d5dba085b037e42f90aaefdec5f6`.

All `OPENAL32.DLL` calls are external API boundaries. The imported library's mixer, device thread and operating-system output are outside the installed `nwmain.exe`. The three `Render` virtual calls at `0x1401e0ca4`, `0x1401e0ff9` and `0x1401e1050` use the source object's slot zero on stop/removal paths. `CExoSoundSourceInternal` constructor `0x1401da910` installs vtable `0x140daed20`; its pinned slot-zero bytes point to deleting destructor `0x1401db1d0`, which calls `ShutDownSource`, destroys the resource helper and conditionally frees the `0x98`-byte object. Wrapper and one-shot constructors call that constructor at `0x1401d9612/0x1401d9695/0x1401df1cc/0x1401dfa3c`; the test helper has two more calls. Thus `0x1401db1d0` is a constructor-backed target for these ordinary sources, while actual list subtype/population and any runtime-added class remain open.
