# NWN-75: original no-BSP and sky Gob rendering

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. These are two distinct entries into `Gob::Render` and recursive `Gob::PartDraw`. The original bodies, direct physical sites, typed vtable sites and known Part subtype candidate targets are in `branch-ledger.json`. The map remains partial where a live model's Part classes or event handlers are not fixed by static callsites.

## No-BSP Scene route

When `Scene::Render` has no BSP pointer, Scene virtual `+0x270` enters `Scene::RenderOnlyGobs` `0x1400cf9b0`. It sets GUI-facing depth/color state and loops Scene Gob array `+0x130` with count `+0x138`. The named `sceneGUI_guiModelView` and `sceneGUI_Viewport_Compass` branches call `LightManager::GetNearestLights` at `0x1400cfbca` before drawing a Gob; the ordinary no-BSP branch skips that nearest-light step. Both invoke Gob virtual `+0x20=Gob::Render` at `0x1400cfb50` or `0x1400cfbdf`. The body has 19 direct sites and two computed Gob sites, now individually ledgered. REA `ev_82cf91e9628c437f02cd36e0ad0c516430379d2d128c9a3ad38d26bb7f6cf7e7`; Gob vtable identity is established in [geometry/draw boundary](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md). This route should not be folded into the BSP draw-bucket path.

## Sky route inside a BSP pass

`Scene::RenderSinglePass` calls `Scene::RenderSkyBoxes` `0x1400d28f0` before transparent bucket 1. Sky geometry setup is skipped when Scene byte `+0x4c` equals 1. Otherwise it disables depth/fog-related state, sets a sky projection from current camera view angle and screen ratio, and conditionally submits up to three Gobs. The first indexed Gob needs Scene `+0x44 != -1` and gets alpha `1 - Scene+0x40`; the second needs positive blend fraction and Scene `+0x48 != -1` and gets that fraction as alpha; a separate Gob at Scene `+0x38` needs a nonnull pointer. Each receives position through Gob virtual `+0xb0=Gob::SetPosition` then renders through `+0x20=Gob::Render`: sites `0x1400d2ad7/0x1400d2ae8`, `0x1400d2b54/0x1400d2b65`, and `0x1400d2bae/0x1400d2bba`. The routine then restores depth, fog, blend, matrix and color state. All 31 direct and six computed sites are now in the ledger (REA `ev_5c0b4f4bf8247888dde227b40f7a2e7a6844f796540b64b67f7ccf94dfc13b18`). The state wrappers' cache/API leaves are documented separately.

## Shared Gob and Part descent

`Gob::Render` `0x1400a4280` begins with `Gob::ProcessGob` at `0x1400a429e`, which conditionally updates cached geometry flags when the Gob dirty byte is set. It can ask Gob events for debug personal radius/path, obtains adjusted pose and inherited attachment transform, pushes a model matrix, applies fog or fade state, and calls `Gob::PartDraw` at `0x1400a44af` or `0x1400a4521`. An optional `param_1` then raises `propagaterender` at `0x1400a4545`. Its 16 direct and nine computed sites are ledgered (REA `ev_d98db872a4486fbaa28cf927d2f19580b49a45a978006606b83a5bb398210770`). The [non-emitter animation/Gob supplement](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md) maps the dirty-byte producer/consumer and the Attach callback that can reenter another Gob `Render(true)`; the active event handler set remains a separate dynamic boundary.

`Gob::PartDraw` `0x1400a3440` checks Part visibility byte `+0x34`. An admitted node pushes/transforms/scales the current matrix, asks Part virtual `+0x58=AsPartTriMesh` at `0x1400a34ae`, calls Part virtual `+0xc8=Draw` at `0x1400a34e7`, recurses over stored Part children at `0x1400a350b`, then pops the matrix. It restores the prior process-global model state on return. Five direct sites plus the two computed sites are in the ledger (REA `ev_5080b9fb6725bb9ac451460297aad6160dfc13a6af388d001037368a792d5233`). The `AsPartTriMesh` site has base-null and mesh-self candidates. The [full RTTI-backed Part Draw census](part-draw-rtti-census.md) finds 18 primary Part-derived tables with 11 distinct Draw slots: the previously mapped ten plus a guarded PartProjection error trampoline. The emitter, Camera and Reference additions inherit empty base Draw. Material/primitive/GL descendants for the actual draw bodies are in [geometry/draw boundary](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md) and [Part draw subclasses](part-draw-subclasses.md). Static evidence does not establish which subtype mix a particular no-BSP or sky Gob contains or exclude RTTI-stripped/external tables.

The no-BSP route's light selection and the sky route's projection are separate upstream conditions, but both share the Gob/Part draw subtree. The remaining dynamic boundaries are the active Part subtype set per model, authored attachment event chain, and possible `Draw` overrides outside the bounded RTTI-backed set.
