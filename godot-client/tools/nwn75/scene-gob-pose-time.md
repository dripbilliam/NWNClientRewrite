# NWN-75: original adjusted Gob pose and interpolation time

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. The Gob vtable `+0x2d0/+0x2d8/+0x2e0` adjusted scale, position and orientation getters are shared by BSP visibility, Part world transforms, shadows, highlights and effects. The animation-side producers/callers are mapped in [non-emitter animation](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md); this document follows their render-visible getter descendants. Exact physical child sites and gates are in `branch-ledger.json`.

`Gob::GetAdjustedScale` `0x14008e390` calls `LerpFloat::Lerped` at `0x14008e3a0` for `Gob+0x1c0`, then multiplies by stored scale `+0x49c` (REA `ev_4689e9616cae2c9f18bb47fbfae9cbd1e654d0b5bbc3a9f5e0f903a794aa0853`). `Gob::GetAdjustedPosition` `0x14008e2c0` calls `Lerped` for three position channels at `0x14008e2e6/2f5/304`, uses scalar quaternion-vector multiplication at `0x14008e32c`, and adds the result to base Gob position `+0xa8/+0xac/+0xb0` (`ev_b205929d8b5c88f0c02bd1bd74b450b12abff5e74a20c485870d80e2129e5934`). `Gob::GetAdjustedOrientation` `0x14008e200` reads three angle interpolators at `0x14008e226/235/244`; only when any angle is nonzero does it call `YawPitchRoll` at `0x14008e282` and quaternion multiplication at `0x14008e296` (`ev_b25a812a39237dc2f7125e38445a3a789f772a37917c0a6896c1a584103255ca`). None of the three directly invokes GL.

`LerpFloat::Lerped` `0x1402e9a80` returns its stored endpoint immediately when mode `+0xc==0`. For active modes it calls `Progress` at `0x1402e9a9a`, handles endpoint progress 0 or 1, and applies one of seven interpolation formulas. Modes 3–5 reach scalar math helpers at `0x1402e9bac/bc4/bfc/c38`; the other admitted formulas are arithmetic in the body (`ev_955c11478108ef90095c17506cc131dc9155309e54a0996879edb7dd2ddd662c`). `YawPitchRoll` `0x14022f6d0` conditionally calls paired scalar angle helpers for nonzero Euler channels and quaternion multiplication for the latter two (`ev_054de2ec6f8f30a19fe5c478699216fa67ca660ac57a854ff11181414f2f581d`). Scalar quaternion-vector `0x14022ae90` and quaternion-quaternion `0x14022ab00` operators have no callees or GL and are terminal CPU leaves (`ev_2bf4955e80ece260cc5d49c4e4bf5bbe6e1e8bdb869b4ec5a2df2cd451942733`, `ev_24da05da46e1662555805b81e0399645de3c861626a6728db05b579fe730978a`).

`LerpFloat::Progress` `0x1402e9cd0` is the time-source boundary. An active microsecond-clock interpolator calls `CExoTimers::GetMicrosecondTimer` at `0x1402e9d18`; an active world-clock interpolator obtains `CAppManager::GetWorldTimer`, reads world time and subtracts world-time pairs at `0x1402e9d8c/9dba/9e20/9e5c`. When the progress-completion/repeat conditions are met, it may swap endpoints and call `LerpFloat::RefreshInternally` at `0x1402e9ed0` (`ev_145e63ebaba4421b01ff0d22ef212e872c422c24445d748b28039cf58039fb5d`). `RefreshInternally` `0x1402ea120` starts a new microsecond interval or computes a world-time interval only for nonzero mode with unequal endpoints; it writes the interpolator's time fields and uses the timer calls at `0x1402ea157/1c9/1ee/200/229` (`ev_cd0d754993bdef2745d7086d02c166a616d147609cacbdf8615cb20e470afec1`). The lower timer-worker/getter chain is documented in [non-emitter animation](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md). Thus a render-facing pose getter can read timer state and, at a transition, update interpolation state; no separate thread is spawned by these getter bodies.
