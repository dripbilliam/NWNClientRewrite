## Geometry and buffer descendants

`HitCheckAABBnode` `0x140090170` box-tests each node, recursively visits both children for internal nodes, and for admitted leaves locks the vertex pool, calls `polyhit` `0x140231810`, and unlocks. `AnimMeshHitCheck` `0x14008bd40` reads dynamic vertex data through `VertexDataObject::GetItemDynamicData` `0x1400bd7a0` and tests indexed faces through `polyhit` `0x140231070`. The latter is a CPU polygon/segment routine with a square-root helper; the `0x140231810` overload has no direct callees in its observed body. The exact recursion, data-getter, polygon and math-helper callsites are in the ledger. Evidence: AABB `ev_541ed45c998a45e12f3c593685700e991d5777b6db85a6d370ec288cfd606385`, AnimMesh `ev_d6c5ec5570fe0b5a8ffb671f11f24329a48d39729589ae5ef1fa5f81e665380f`, polygon overloads `ev_a21d6e7b7e5bf1ccc08b188f23ab7ee78660fa3bbaea18076df77dba747ab0b7` and `ev_b7c99dad4a1d6e0c1d88e417d34a0fd59804f4b4b6a02f4de5e342e8c0cc8e57`.

`RenderInterface::LockPool` `0x1400bee30` tail-jumps to `GLRender::LockPool` `0x1400b4aa0`, which returns a CPU pool pointer and records the caller's mode at descriptor `+0x11`. The [TriMesh geometry continuation](original-hitcheck-geometry.md) establishes a read-mode `0x18` lock before its `UnlockPool(poolId, size=-1, offset=0)` at `0x140090b67`; the [other geometry continuation](original-hitcheck-other-geometry.md) establishes read-mode `8` for the AABB leaf. `GLRender::UnlockPool` prepends its manager pointer, so `size=-1` is nonzero in helper `0x1400bbaf0`; the **mode mask** `+0x11 & 0x18` is what skips its conditional `glBufferData`/`glBufferSubData` branch for these hit-test releases. AnimMesh and Trigger tests have no direct GL call. Write-mode callers of the same helper can publish GPU bytes under their separate pool-state gates.
