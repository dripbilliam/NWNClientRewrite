# NWN-75: original projected geometry preparation connector

Pinned original `nwmain.exe` SHA-256 `3b7cb1252e0edb2ce22d7971f333aade027039ae30a45b4bc64732c3e6bec73a`. This is the shared projection setup reached from `VertexPrimitiveFlat::SetupArrays` and, when needed, `PartTriMesh::Project` in the shadow-volume path. The separate [skin/projection supplement](original-scene-animation-nonemitter.md) traces the same body and its model lifecycle; this connector gives exact scene-ledger callsites without claiming a second projection implementation.

`VertexPrimitiveFlat::SetupProjectionArrays` `0x1400acc90` returns when completion flag `+0x74` is set. Otherwise it invokes primitive subtype tests `+0x18` at `0x1400accc6` (Anim null/self candidates), `+0x20` at `0x1400accd2` (Dangly null/self), `+0x10` at `0x1400accdb` (Skin null/self), and owner Part `+0x58` at `0x1400acce8` (base null or TriMesh self). The static projection build proceeds only when the accepted source/model geometry and projection counts are nonzero; it caps the projected count at `0x10000`. The candidate targets are constructor-backed in [primitive/VDO preparation](primitive-vdo-preparation.md) and the skin supplement. The active subtype at a callsite depends on the model. Full body REA `ev_466711929489fc7e2ab85a2f09c053e359f9876fb091b4572062f648f428f225`.

For a qualifying rebuild it removes any prior projection VDO item at `0x1400acd29`, locks the source pool at `0x1400acd8c`, obtains or creates a type-2 VDO token at `0x1400acdc0`, adds an item at `0x1400acdd2`, gets its static CPU data at `0x1400acde0`, writes transformed projection vertices plus two extra vertices, commits static item bytes at `0x1400acf22`, unlocks the source pool at `0x1400acf39`, and sets `+0x74`. `MakeMatrix4x4` and two matrix/vector operations are also site-backed at `0x1400ace30/0x1400ace64/0x1400acecf`. The body has 11 direct and four computed physical callsites (eight subtype target records). Its own work is CPU preparation and conditional pool commit; it has no direct GL draw. The VDO manager/AddItem/commit and pool `UnlockPool` branches can reach conditional GL buffer publication, mapped in [primitive/VDO preparation](primitive-vdo-preparation.md) and [geometry/draw boundary](geometry-and-draw-boundary.md).

`PartTriMesh::Project` calls this primitive slot only when its existing projection VDO is absent, and may then flush the shared shadow element stream through `Scene::RenderDynamicShadows` if it needs capacity. Those gates and the later shadow draw are in [shadows and dynamic geometry](shadows-and-dynamic.md). This connector does not imply projected arrays are rebuilt on every ordinary Part draw.
