# NWN-84 Video artwork admission

The Video Options button now waits for its own original asset cohort before it can be used. `native_video_options.gd` enumerates its requested source models, authored TextureRects, all button poses, applied fonts and current scrollbar thumb. Godot's two internal ScrollContainer TextureRects are excluded because they have no native art key. The gate names an exact failed image or font, and global unrelated UI queues no longer hold this page. It scans during preparation and stops after readiness or failure; a settings change rearms the check. `native_main_menu.gd` receives the completion signal in both synchronous and staged setup, disables the Video affordance until ready, and rejects a direct early action.

Source files: `systems/native_video_options.gd`, `systems/native_main_menu.gd`. New fixtures: `tools/nwn84/video-cohort-gate-test.gd`, `video-cohort-original-test.gd`, `video-cohort-menu-test.gd`.
