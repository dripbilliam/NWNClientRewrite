# NWN-64 hot-loop source gate, 2026-11-12

The retained original G2 subphase capture (`reports/nwn64-current-heavy-subphase-g2-b.native.json`, successful particle samples at 1,829 sources) shows about 3,005 active particles. The sampled diagnostic phase means are simulation 0.676 ms, eligibility 0.406 ms, emitter pose 0.282 ms, channels 0.244 ms, and source pose 0.172 ms. These probes add per-emitter timers and do not establish a normal-client saving.

I inspected `particle_presentation::Owner::frame`, `Step::advance`, `compact_particle::animate/standard` and `Bridge`. The initially promising `standard` loop calls `clear_hit()` once per moving particle even without bounce, but `tools/nwn64/prepare.cjs` and `g2-subphase-diagnostic.md` document the earlier coalescing attempt and its rejection for changing service-call count/order. The present `resident::set(..., NumericArray<3>)` already reuses exclusively owned inline vector storage, so replacing its vector construction is not a fresh allocation win. The G2 scene has zero bounce sources, about five births per full-scene sample, and 670 chunk sources. The source-pose repeated-ID candidate was previously slower in a component check; 0.172 ms is its entire measured upper bound in this capture. Eligibility already uses the direct scene ABI; the 0.283 ms patch/detonate diagnostic is dominated by probe overhead when there are zero patch boundaries.

I found no new substantial, exact-order redundant per-particle operation that passes the source gate. The small pure arithmetic opportunities (conditional quaternion turn, repeated `vertices()` reads, redundant static clip assignment) have no measured cost and do not justify a production edit. Production sources, accepted DLL and normal-client selection remain unchanged; NWN-64 remains in progress.
