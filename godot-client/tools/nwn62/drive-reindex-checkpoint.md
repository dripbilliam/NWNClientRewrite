The saved Tracy `newertrace` chain attributes the longest asset completion callback to one `drive_collision_indexed.gd reindex_sources()` call: `completed.emit` reached 75.006 ms and the nested reindex reached 74.936 ms. ## Candidate scope and results

The private candidate stages the same source key order, spatial completion and 463 static group completions across `_process` callbacks. Until completion, `ready()` and collision queries are fenced. Source edits, motion, the fourth table arriving during full-state receipt, scope retirement and cancellation/restart have exact output/order checks against a frozen pre-edit source.

`tools/nwn62/drive-reindex-component-boundary-c.json` passed 75/75 headless SceneTree checks. Its authored captured shape has 1,918 sources, 1,908 static entries, 463 groups and 10 global entries. In its same-process ABBA, control completion took 39.0/47.1 ms. Candidate completion took 16–18 µs, then 15–16 callbacks with maximum 3.521–3.522 ms. Candidate summed CPU was 46.7/50.7 ms versus control's 39.0/47.1 ms. Thus the callback shrank, but total work increased and readiness may shift about 0.25 seconds at 60 FPS. This fixture does not establish original-asset loading or FPS performance.

The staged path is eligible only for at most 2,048 sources and 1,600 creatures, and for groups of at most 512 entries. Every fallback records a count and reason in `drive_collision.reindex_fallbacks`:

## Synchronous source attribution and exact-work candidate

The frozen synchronous source was decomposed in `drive-reindex-phase-profile-a.json`. Two profiled runs matched the ordinary final query/order byte signature. The 1,918-source reindex spent about 31–32 ms refreshing source rows, about 10 ms completing 463 static group indexes, less than 0.1 ms completing the global index, and 0.8–0.9 ms updating group bounds. Instrumented source detail in `drive-reindex-phase-profile-b.json` places about 21 ms inside static `publish_binding()` and 6 ms inside `descriptor()`; per-call clocks add overhead to that diagnostic. A private incremental-new-group variant preserved final output but increased the whole callback, so it was rejected.

The narrow `drive_collision_indexed_lazy_model.gd` candidate avoids evaluating `Rules.cell(data,"Model","")` when `ModelName` is present. The original nested function call evaluates that fallback eagerly, including a case-insensitive scan when `Model` is absent. The candidate preserves exact-key precedence, mixed-case lookup order, null values, empty values and `****`. `drive-reindex-lazy-model-b.json` passed 8,455 checks: final captured and dense output/order, 10 synthetic edge rows, 17 installed placeable metadata rows, and 8,400 installed door metadata rows. The captured-shape whole reindex averaged about 40.9 ms candidate versus 41.4 ms control across same-process ABBA; dense averaged 31.2 versus 32.1 ms. This is a small exact-work reduction, independent of the staged candidate. Coordinator review approved this narrow edit. Production `drive_collision_indexed.gd` is now SHA256 `f3c2b35e783dd7d0dec24a1978a6f47edc2e3024ef2f37d8b158b6a8cfaca928`; `drive-reindex-lazy-model-production-c.json` passed the same 8,455 parity checks against frozen prior source.
