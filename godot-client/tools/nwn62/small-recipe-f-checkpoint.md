# NWN-62 targeted small-recipe F — private correctness checkpoint

F is a private source overlay, not an accepted renderer change. It chooses the existing E recipe implementation only for five original light-bearing small models when both resource name and decoded model-byte SHA-256 match. Other small models, including changed versions of those five, retain the accepted PackedScene route; the existing large-model recipe route and its unsupported-graph fallback remain. The selector is opt-in with `--model-template=recipe-small-tail-f`.

Saved diagnostic reports attributed the severe recurring stalls to `PackedScene.pack`, not mesh installation. The original-byte peaks were `ttu01_a02_03` 606–834 ms in four runs, `ttu01_l07_02` 391–455 ms in nine, `ttu01_v06_01` 616–704 ms in three, `ttu01_w07_01` 629 ms in one, and `ttu01_h17_01` 799 ms in one. Source hashes for these five agree with the installed-asset census. They each have two light features and an AABB, but `ttu01_v07_01` has the same feature pattern and a much smaller observed 57 ms peak. That feature pattern cannot safely distinguish expensive from cheap graphs. F is therefore a bounded original-asset exception, not a general structural selector.

E selected 110–111 light templates and created 600 recipe clones, versus 15 recipe templates and 401 recipe clones in its controls. E removed the worst observed template pack tail but delayed known-base completion by 305 and 658 ms. F limits extra recipe eligibility to at most these five original source identities; its actual scene clone count and loading impact remain unmeasured.
