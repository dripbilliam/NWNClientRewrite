No source, runtime or FPS work changed.

`AssetService._profile_process()` checks completion count/time *before* `completed.emit`; the nested consumer remains indivisible. The profiler-on `reports/nwn62-small-recipe-d-control-1.engine.log` contains an actual `but_chat_reply` actor-model completion of 58.948 ms, 58.915 ms in `consumer_us`, a `doortypes` 2DA completion of 41.830 ms, 41.808 ms in the consumer, and `gui_qbar_ends` of 11.795 ms, 11.780 ms in its consumer. Worker join and sidecar times are only single-digit microseconds in those rows. These are exact callback observations for that replay, not a stable live distribution.

`doortypes` reaches the full `drive_collision_indexed.gd reindex_sources()` path. The prior captured-shape split reduced the synchronous callback but added total CPU and about 0.25 s of staged readiness in its component; live p99 movement worsened. Repeating that candidate would not satisfy the requested total-work and loading gates. The texture phase probe separately measured a 12.199 ms `ImageTexture.create_from_image` miss. Its worker-created-texture candidate later created 626 speculative textures but used none in profiler-on whole-scene runs, with 418/1,307 ms slower known-base completion. A generic worker move is therefore also not a quick accepted fix.

`but_chat_reply` is requested by `native_chat_hud.gd:configure/_process`; when its completion drains the final job, `prepared()` synchronously calls `activate()`. That method parses layout and reply data, constructs and binds two pane trees and a prompt/mode/context owner, then calls `refresh_modes`, `apply_message_font`, `place`, `rebuild_admission` and `render_history` before `context_prepared.emit`. `render_history` can recreate rich text, reply buttons and offsets from the current chat rows. The saved callback counter does not isolate which of those subcalls dominates. A `call_deferred(activate)` would move the same 59 ms to another frame without splitting it; yielding between the node-construction statements would expose partially initialized panes unless state/admission and cancellation are redesigned. The 11.795 ms quickbar completion similarly reaches UI consumer work, with no measured leaf yet.

There is no proven one-file, small, safe split of an individually >10 ms operation in this bounded evidence. Before a production edit, add phase clocks around the chat activation subcalls (especially `render_history` and art binding), plus a same-source callback-tail report; choose a resumable boundary only if it preserves partial-owner cleanup, admission and exact first visible output. Keep NWN-62 In Progress. Do not infer FPS or promote the rejected staging/worker variants.
