## Validation and limits

Focused branch tests cover heartbeat/perception boundaries, RNG order, spawn and associate branches, reputation/invitation expiry, script locking, unsigned IDs, AI cursor/order/budgets, event15 deferral, overflow and level0 delivery retention. Live runtime and [original perception observations](combat-native-perception.md) agree for the claimed ordinary NPC scope. Normal started sessions use the native AI traversal; startup-disabled resource/owner tests retain their explicit bypass.

The local monotonic microsecond source and scheduler deadlines are adapters, not a replay of original server uptime/CPU costs. Active area clock selection, full native object elapsed-time initialization, module DoUpdate calendar/heartbeat, UpdateDialog timing, automap, height/trap updates and general noncreature AI registration remain pending. The existing local round/action delta adapter remains explicitly partial. Original RNG and complete encounters have not been replayed. These limitations prevent a combat-ready claim.
